Tools

  • K-Map generatorKarnaugh map solver for 2 to 6 variables: groups, SOP and POS, truth table, Verilog, VHDL and the circuit.
  • Cache simulatorDirect-mapped, set-associative and fully associative caches with LRU, FIFO and random replacement, stepped one access at a time.
  • IEEE 754 converterHalf, single and double precision. Encode a value, decode a bit pattern, flip bits.
  • Bit manipulation visualizerBitwise expressions evaluated step by step on 4 to 32-bit values.
  • Adder architecture visualizerRipple carry, carry lookahead, carry select and carry save, with carries animated and area against delay.
  • Multiplier architecture visualizerShift-and-add, Booth radix-2, array and Wallace tree, with partial product reduction stepped through.
  • CircuitGenGate-level circuit diagrams from Boolean expressions, exported as SVG.
  • Order book simulatorA limit order book with price-time priority matching, stepped one operation at a time.

Projects

  • CalscriptA C++17 command-line calculator. The parser uses the shunting-yard algorithm.
  • VokunA package bundle manager for pacman-based Linux. Bundles are TOML with inheritance, optional packages and post-install hooks.

About

The tools here started as study aids for my own coursework. When a textbook diagram was not enough, I built the thing and stepped through it.

At UTA I am a member of the National Society of Black Engineers and the Association for Computing Machinery. I was part of Freshman Leaders on Campus in my first year and served as a student advocate for the CAHSI chapter.

I want to work on hard problems in mathematics and hardware, and I am always looking for chances to learn and to apply what I know.
